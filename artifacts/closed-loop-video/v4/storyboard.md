## Visual rules

- Product footage occupies at least 88 percent of live-action frames.
- Stage cards last 2.5 seconds with at least 1.4 seconds at full opacity.
- The source image appears full frame at the opening model reveal and close.
- Product copy must remain readable on a 13-inch laptop at normal playback size.
- Evidence callouts contain one claim and remain on screen long enough to read.
- Cursor movement follows the real interaction path and pauses on evidence.
- Cursor paths are scene-specific and click cues coincide with the visible control.
- Dashboard footage must never play faster than its recorded speed.
- Context cards appear one at a time and contain one claim each.
- Verify and Prevent use a thin gold focus box to link each note to its evidence.
- All assets are normalized to Rec.709, 1920 by 1080, square pixels, and 30 fps.
- The master uses CRF 17 with H.264 High profile and a UI-safe encode.
