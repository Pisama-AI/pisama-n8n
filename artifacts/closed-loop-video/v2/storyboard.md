## Editing notes

- Use a quiet 250 to 300 millisecond dip to black when changing chapters.
- Keep cursor motion imperfect but purposeful: short acceleration, a small overshoot, then a correction before each click.
- Let meaningful UI states hold for at least 1.5 seconds.
- Keep the full product frame for orientation, then let panel openings and scrolling provide the detail.
- Add click sounds only to state-changing actions. Keep them 18 to 22 dB below narration.
- Keep the music bed textural and nearly subliminal. Remove it during the first timeout reveal.
