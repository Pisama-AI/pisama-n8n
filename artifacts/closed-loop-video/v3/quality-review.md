## What keeps it below the 90s

- There is no on-camera human introduction or closing shot. The human presence comes from interaction pacing and first-person narration.
- Product footage is framed inside the image-led stage layout, so small interface copy is less legible than in a full-screen technical walkthrough.
- The narration is generated, not a recorded founder or operator voice.
- The prevention chapter proves one concrete data-contract guardrail lifecycle. It does not establish prevention across every detector class.
- The final prevention record is intentionally rolled back after verification. This is honest and auditable, but a production deployment montage would create a stronger commercial finish.
