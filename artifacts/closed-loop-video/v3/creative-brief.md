# Image-led creative brief

## Subject, audience, and job

Subject: the PISAMA closed reliability loop for n8n.

Audience: automation engineers and engineering leaders responsible for releasing workflows that can fail outside the happy path.

Single job: make the supplied six-stage reliability-loop graphic feel like the product's operating model, then prove each stage with live n8n and PISAMA evidence.

## Visual system derived from the source image

- Void: `#0b0d0b`
- Grid: `#1a1e1a`
- Panel: `#1a1d1a`
- Ivory: `#f3efe4`
- Utility gray: `#999386`
- Reliability gold: `#e9ad28`

Georgia carries stage titles and conclusions. Arial carries explanatory copy. The system monospace face carries stage numbers, evidence values, and detector labels.

The image's square grid becomes the 16:9 canvas. Its six numbered cards become chapter bumpers. The gold arrows become a progress path, and the supplied image remains visible as a small loop map beside the live product interface.

## Signature

The memorable element is the gold orbit: the highlighted stage advances clockwise around the source loop, then the matching product action occupies the main frame.

## Deliberate risk and critique

The source image is used as the film's navigation system instead of a decorative insert. This creates a stronger identity, but it can become repetitive. The edit limits each stage bumper to roughly two seconds and uses the live interface as the dominant visual surface.

The image says “diagnose + heal” and “prevent recurrence.” The video may use those labels only where the product evidence supports them. A human review is not called a repair. A regression score is not called prevention. The final stage requires a real guardrail lifecycle or is explicitly labeled as an unproven next action.
